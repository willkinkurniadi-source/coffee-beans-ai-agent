# WILLKIN COFFEE ROASTERY — Brand Guidelines v1.0

## Brand Statement
**Premium Indonesian Coffee, Built for the World.**

WILLKIN is a specialty coffee roastery from Indonesia, committed to delivering exceptional quality, consistency, and long-term value to the global market.

## Brand Essence
- **Vision:** Become one of the most respected Indonesian coffee roasteries globally.
- **Mission:** Deliver carefully roasted Indonesian coffee with global standards and consistent quality.
- **Tagline (primary):** Built Around Coffee

## Core Values
- **Precision** — Every roast matters.
- **Craftsmanship** — Quality over shortcuts.
- **Trust** — Long-term relationships over short-term profit.
- **Origin** — Indonesia is not decoration. It is identity.

## Brand Personality
| WHAT WILLKIN IS | WHAT WILLKIN IS NOT |
|---|---|
| Sophisticated | Trendy cafe aesthetic |
| Confident | Cartoonish |
| Expert | Overly rustic |
| Global | Cheap-looking |
| Premium | Too many elements |
| Minimal | Coffee addict culture |
| Mature | |

## Target Customer
**Primary:** Specialty buyers, Coffee enthusiasts, Export buyers, Hotels & Restaurants, Offices, Premium retail buyers
**Secondary:** People upgrading from mass market coffee

## Color Palette
| Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Coffee Brown | `#2A1F17` | Primary dark, backgrounds, text |
| Soft White | `#F8F7F4` | Page background, light surfaces |
| Warm Cream | `#ECE5DA` | Secondary background, cards |
| Gold Accent | `#B7925C` | Accent only — CTA, highlights |

## Typography
- **Primary Font:** Serif — Elegant, Timeless, Confident. High contrast serif for premium feel.
- **Secondary Font:** Sans-serif — Clean, Readable, Modern. Used for body text and information.

## Tone of Voice
Speak like: **Luxury watch brand + Professional roastery**

- ❌ BAD: "Our coffee is super delicious!!"
- ✅ GOOD: "Carefully roasted to highlight the character of Indonesian coffee."
